Education through discovery

An independently developed collection of illustrated educational publications exploring structure, patterns, mathematics and symbolic systems through observation, comparison and hands-on discovery.

Created by independent researcher Ashley Iannetti through Iannetti Studies, the collection encourages curiosity, critical thinking and independent investigation. The original symbolic and mathematical investigations also helped motivate later research in biological acoustic signaling, published in BioSystems in 2026.

The publications present selected educational concepts, not the complete research archive or unpublished methods.
