Peer-reviewed research

In 2026, independent research by Ashley Iannetti was published in the peer-reviewed journal BioSystems (Elsevier).

A Conserved Systems-Level Constraint in Biological Acoustic Signaling

The study presents a comparative systems analysis of biological acoustic signaling using a normalized frequency representation designed for scale-independent comparison.

The analysis identified a consistent, non-uniform mean energy profile that was qualitatively reproducible across the locked dataset and remained stable under bin-resolution changes and leave-one-recording-out analyses.

The work establishes a quantitative framework for investigating conserved systems-level organization in biological acoustic signaling, with explicitly defined controls, boundary conditions, null models, and falsifiable predictions for further testing in larger and independent datasets.

Author: Ashley Iannetti

Journal: BioSystems (Elsevier)

Volume: 263, 105758 (2026)

Applying the Rosetta Wheel Framework Across Signal Domains

The Rosetta Wheel Framework is an independent research framework developed by Ashley Iannetti through Iannetti Studies. It explores whether structural relationships within complex systems can be compared through normalization and shared reference mapping.

Within this framework, Normalized Frequency Space (NFS) is used to transform signals into a common analytical space, allowing structural relationships to be explored independently of their original scale.

ROSETTA WHEEL originated from independent investigations into patterns, organization, and structural relationships across historical, symbolic, and mathematical systems.

These earlier investigations later helped motivate research into biological acoustic communication, leading to a peer-reviewed scientific publication in BioSystems in 2026.

The published biological study represents one research direction within a much broader investigation. Much of the detailed underlying mathematical research, technical methodology, and additional cross-system work remains unpublished.

About

Iannetti Studies is an independent research project led by Ashley Iannetti, focused on identifying structural and mathematical constraints that recur across biological signaling systems, symbolic systems, and complex signal environments.

The current public work documents the Rosetta Wheel framework and related cross-domain structural methods. Additional applications are being developed across bioacoustics, physiological signals, neural signal analysis, engineering datasets, and broader signal-processing problems.

The purpose of Iannetti Studies is to document, test, and develop cross-domain structural methods while preserving authorship, research records, and future collaboration opportunities.
